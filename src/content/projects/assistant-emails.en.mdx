## Context & Stakes

A business executive receives an average of **50 emails per day** requiring a response. Manual processing — sorting, reading, drafting, sending — took **2 hours per day (10h/week)** on low-value tasks delaying important decisions.

Volume grew without matching processing capacity: **missed follow-ups**, **mood-dependent responses**, inbox stress impacting overall productivity.

## Technical Solution

The AI assistant integrates **directly into the existing email client**, requiring zero behavior change. RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) architecture leverages history for tone consistency.

### 3-Step Pipeline

1. **Reading & Analysis** — Classification by intent (info request, action request, retention) and priority
2. **Response Generation** — Pre-filled replies contextualized (conversation history + executive's past responses)
3. **Human Validation** — Executive validates, edits, or rejects in one click. **Zero sends without validation** (non-negotiable safeguard)

### Stack & Architecture

- **RAG** for contextual retrieval (last 5 exchanges per conversation)
- **Auto language detection** (FR/EN) with same-language reply
- **Non-invasive integration** into native mail client

## Technical Challenges & Solutions

| Challenge | Solution |
|-----------|----------|
| Initial response quality (missing context) | Prompt enriched with last 5 exchanges per conversation |
| Multilingual emails (FR/EN) | Language detection + generation in same language |
| Non-disruptive integration | Mandatory human validation circuit — zero auto-send |

## Results & Impact

| Metric | Before | After |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Processing time | 2h/day | ~25min/day |
| Emails processed | 50+/day | 50+/day (same volume) |
| Missed follow-ups | Frequent | Zero (automated tracking) |
| Tone consistency | Variable (mood) | Consistent (RAG) |

**Net gain: ~1h35/day recovered** for high-value work. Mental load reduced, zero misses, guaranteed consistency.
