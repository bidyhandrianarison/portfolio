## Context

Team meetings generate decisions, actions, and responsibilities that need to be tracked. In many organizations, writing meeting minutes is a manual post-meeting task, often rushed due to time constraints.

## Problem

The executive spent 30 minutes after each meeting drafting minutes. Some decisions were forgotten, follow-up actions were not always assigned, and the lack of reliable written records created team friction.

## Approach

I developed a tool that transforms meeting notes into structured documents:

1. **Structured input** — During the meeting, participants record key points in a guided format (decision, action, owner, deadline).
2. **AI analysis** — The system automatically identifies decisions, follow-up actions, and responsible parties from free-form notes.
3. **Minutes generation** — A structured document is produced in 30 seconds, with clear sections: summary, decisions, actions, next steps.

## Challenges

- **Heterogeneous notes** — Note-taking styles vary enormously between individuals. The model was trained on hundreds of real notes to handle this variability.
- **Decision vs. action distinction** — Some items are decisions (to remember) while others are actions (to do). The system learns to distinguish them through context and linguistic markers.
- **Confidentiality** — Minutes contain sensitive information. The tool runs locally, without sending data to external services.

## Result

Drafting dropped from 30 minutes to 30 seconds. All decisions are now captured and tracked. Follow-up actions are automatically assigned and monitored, reducing missed items to zero.
